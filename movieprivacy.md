Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Who We Are

BoxMax is operated by Brave Studio (“moviebox,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a company registered in the United Kingdom. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains what information we collect when you use BoxMax and how we handle it.

What We Collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. The information we may collect falls into two categories:

Account details you choose to provide, such as an email address used for your bookings.
Anonymous usage and diagnostic data, which helps us keep the app fast, stable, and reliable.
How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to run and improve BoxMax. Specifically, we use it to:

Show you cinema showtimes near you
Process and deliver your ticket bookings
Find and fix bugs
Understand which features people find useful

We do not sell your personal information.

Movie Data from TMDB

Movie details in moviebox, including titles, descriptions, posters, ratings, and cast information, are provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). This content is governed by TMDB’s own terms of use and privacy policy. We do not share your personal information with TMDB.

BoxMax uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Advertising and Tracking

BoxMax is completely ad-free. We do not show ads, and we do not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. We never sell or share your personal information with advertisers, and we never use your data for ad targeting.

Your Choices

You can use most of BoxMax without creating an account. If you do have an account, you can ask us to delete your account data at any time by contacting us.
